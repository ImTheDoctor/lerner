# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: React with Vite, chosen for a lightweight static invitation that can be run locally and deployed as a static site.

## Users

Wedding guests visiting from desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.

## Product Purpose

An immersive Armenian wedding invitation presented as a field journal / expedition log. Success means guests can understand the invitation, event details, and practical guidance comfortably on any screen while experiencing the approved mountain-journal art direction.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Five semantic sections: hero, invitation, expedition details, mountain gallery, and final invitation. Smooth scrolling, subtle reveal motion, responsive layout, Armenian Unicode support, accessible HTML, and reduced-motion support are required. No RSVP workflow, invented logistics, or additional factual event details.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the approved vintage expedition journal aesthetic: warm parchment, forest green typography and stamps, Armenian mountain landscapes, topographic contour lines, torn paper transitions, hiking photo collage, and a warm sunset closing section.

## Evidence on Hand

Approved desktop and mobile visual references: `exec-a6663976-d006-4849-b396-bba0e120a59c.png` and `exec-546ecfe8-8dd9-441e-a126-d5c865930878.png`. The original Armenian invitation text is supplied in the approved references and is reproduced in the page unchanged.

## Product Principles

- The invitation should feel like a keepsake field journal, not a generic wedding template.
- Practical guest information stays legible and easy to scan.
- Armenian copy remains intact and is never translated, shortened, or rewritten.
- Every visual asset is independent so the page remains responsive and accessible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, descriptive image alt text, visible focus states, keyboard-accessible navigation, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
